OUR HERITAGE

Three generations
at the bride's side.

Maison DAHAB carries the rituals of Moroccan negafa — the styling, the henna, the seven gowns, the ammariya — with the rigor of a couture atelier and the warmth of a family that welcomes you home.

From Casablanca to Paris, from Brussels to Dubai, we dress the brides who choose to wear Morocco with majesty, and the present with grace.
